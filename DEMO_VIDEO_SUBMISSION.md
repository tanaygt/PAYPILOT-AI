# PayPilot AI — YouTube demo plan (2:30)

## Recording setup

- Use the local app at `http://localhost:5173`.
- Record the browser window and microphone with any screen recorder already installed.
- Do not show `.env`, PayPal secrets, Groq keys, or browser account details.
- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** or **Public**, with no password.

## Exact narration and shots

**0:00–0:15 — Problem**

“Small teams know what they need, but procurement is slow and budget control is fragile. PayPilot AI turns a plain-English brief into a controlled purchase.” Show the dashboard and the `$0.00` verified-spend state.

**0:15–0:40 — Request**

Open AI Assistant and submit: “I need two monitors and one office chair. My total budget is $600. Prioritize quality and value.”

**0:40–1:10 — AI plan**

Show the matched Acer monitors and Branch chair, quantities, rationale, `$597.00` total, and `$3.00 remaining`. Say: “The server owns the catalogue and recalculates the total. AI recommends; it does not authorize.”

**1:10–1:35 — PayPal approval**

Show the PayPal Sandbox button. Click it and use the Sandbox personal buyer account. Do not show credentials. Say: “The order is created server-side only after review, and the capture is verified server-side.”

**1:35–1:55 — Success and audit trail**

After approval, open Transactions and show the completed transaction, amount, date, PayPal order ID, and capture reference.

**1:55–2:15 — Safety**

Show the Catalogue and explain server-side prices, budget enforcement, rate limiting, and the explicit approval gate. Briefly show an over-budget request being blocked.

**2:15–2:30 — Close**

“PayPilot AI is controlled agentic commerce: faster procurement, transparent recommendations, and human authorization for every payment.”
